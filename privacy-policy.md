# Privacy Policy for Tenfold

**Last updated:** August 2026

Tenfold ("the app") is a private, local-first gratitude journal. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data Collection

Tenfold does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no backend of any kind.

## Where Your Data Lives

All journal entries — including any text, photos, audio recordings, and videos you add — are stored locally on your device using the browser's IndexedDB storage, inside the app's private sandbox. This data:

- Is never transmitted off your device
- Is never shared with the developer or any third party
- Is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage

## Permissions Used

- **Notifications / Exact Alarms** — used only to deliver the daily reminders you configure in Settings. No data leaves your device to schedule or show these.
- **Microphone** — used only if you choose to record an in-app voice note for an entry. The recording is saved locally like any other entry media.
- **Internet** — included by the underlying app framework but not used to send your journal data anywhere.

## Media Sharing

If you choose to share an entry or a trophy image (e.g., to Instagram Story or WhatsApp), Tenfold generates an image on your device and hands it to the Android share sheet you select. That image is only sent wherever you personally choose to send it.

## Children's Privacy

Tenfold does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
